# 🚦 Urban Traffic Analysis Dashboard

A modern and interactive **Power BI dashboard** focused on analyzing urban traffic patterns, congestion levels, accident trends, and transportation insights to support smarter city planning and data-driven decision making.

# 📌 Project Overview

The **Urban Traffic Analysis Dashboard** transforms raw transportation and traffic datasets into actionable insights using advanced data visualization techniques in Power BI.

This project helps stakeholders:

- Monitor traffic congestion levels
- Identify accident-prone zones
- Analyze vehicle movement trends
- Track peak traffic hours
- Understand road utilization patterns
- Improve urban transportation planning

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Urban areas face increasing traffic congestion, accidents, and inefficient transportation systems. Traditional reports often fail to provide real-time and visual insights required for faster decision-making.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by offering:

- Centralized traffic analytics
- Interactive filtering and drill-down analysis
- KPI-based performance tracking
- Data-driven urban planning support

# 📈 Key Insights Generated

- Peak congestion primarily occurs during office commute hours.
- Certain zones consistently experience heavy traffic density.
- Accident frequency increases during high congestion periods.
- Heavy vehicle movement contributes significantly to slower traffic flow.
- Traffic patterns vary considerably based on time and location.
